# Tswana Petroleum Co. — Brand Guidelines

The source-of-truth brand system that the website (`/web`) is built from.

## Color Palette

| Role | Name | HEX | RGB | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | Tswana Teal | `#00A3A1` | 0, 163, 161 | Logos, headers, CTAs, primary accents |
| Secondary | Deep Navy | `#002855` | 0, 40, 85 | Body text, footers, dark backgrounds |
| Accent 1 | Energy Green | `#00C4B3` | 0, 196, 179 | Highlights, hover states, gradients |
| Accent 2 | Flame Amber | `#F5A623` | 245, 166, 35 | Alerts, fuel-type icons, energy motifs |
| Neutral Light | Cloud White | `#F4F7F9` | 244, 247, 249 | Backgrounds, card surfaces |
| Neutral Dark | Carbon Grey | `#2D3436` | 45, 52, 54 | Secondary text, borders |

**Gradient Signature:** `#002855 → #00A3A1 → #00C4B3` (hero banners and key visuals).

## Identity

- **Name:** Tswana Petroleum Co.
- **Recommended tagline:** *Powering South Africa. Fuelling the Future.*
- **Voice:** Authoritative yet approachable · technically precise · proudly South African with pan-African ambition · sustainability-conscious.

## The TSWANA Pillars

- **T** — Trust
- **S** — Safety
- **W** — Wholesale Excellence
- **A** — Accountability
- **N** — Next-Gen Energy
- **A** — African Pride
